# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard created to analyze Vrinda Store's annual sales and order performance for 2022.

The dashboard helps understand monthly sales trends, customer demographics, order status, state-wise sales performance, and sales channels.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers and Interactive Filters
* Data Cleaning and Data Visualization

## Dashboard Preview

![Vrinda Store Annual Sales Dashboard](images/dashboard-preview.png)

## Key Performance Areas

* **Monthly Sales and Orders:** Compares monthly revenue and order counts.
* **Sales by Gender:** Analyzes the contribution of men and women to sales.
* **Order Status:** Displays delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
* **Top 5 States:** Compares sales performance across leading states.
* **Age and Gender Analysis:** Examines customer demographics.
* **Sales Channels:** Compares orders from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, and other channels.

## Key Insights

* Women account for approximately 64% of sales, compared with 36% for men.
* Approximately 92% of orders are delivered.
* Amazon represents the largest channel share in the dashboard, at approximately 35%.
* Maharashtra is the highest-selling state among the five states displayed.
* Monthly sales and order counts vary throughout the year.

*Note: Percentages and findings are based on the displayed dashboard and should be validated against the underlying workbook.*
